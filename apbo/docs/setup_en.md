# AP_Bo Setup

1. Put `apbo.apworld` in your Archipelago `custom_worlds` folder.
2. Generate a template from the Launcher ("Generate Template Options") and edit `AP_Bo.yaml`.
3. After generating and hosting, open **AP_Bo Client** from the Launcher and connect.
4. Use the **AP Bo** tab: click a table, click a card, click a build pile.

Commands also work in the console: `/tables`, `/play <n>`, `/p <src> <build>`,
`/discard <hand> <pile>`, `/hint`, `/undo`, `/auto`, `/autogame`, `/autostock`,
`/mulligan`, `/wild`, `/forfeit`, `/status`, `/store`, `/buy <n>`.
