How to play

Build piles go 1 to 12; a wild (W) counts as whatever is needed. Play from your hand, the top of your discard piles, or your stockpile. Empty your stockpile first to win.

Tap a card to select it; the piles it fits glow green. Tap one to play it, or tap the selected card again to send it to the first pile that fits. To end your turn, select a hand card and tap one of your discard piles.

Keys: 1-9 hand · S stockpile · H hint · U undo · A auto turn · Esc deselect.
